Hidden Markov model: which coin did Jack throw?

Jack has two coins that look identical. The fair coin F shows head (0) or tail (1) at 50%; the cheat coin C shows head at 90% and tail at 10%. If he has just used the fair coin he picks it again with probability 0.9; if he has just used the cheat coin he keeps it with probability 0.8. After choosing a coin he throws it and only tells us the outcome. He repeats this many times, and we want to guess which coin he used at each throw. When e(0|C) is unknown, we also want to estimate it from the throws. Changing a parameter re-simulates with the same seed; edit the observation to decode your own throws.
